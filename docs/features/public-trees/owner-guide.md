# Owner Guide: Public Family Trees

## 1. Publishing a Family Tree
1. Open your Family Tree and navigate to **Cài đặt dòng họ** (Settings).
2. Scroll to section **3. Chế độ công khai cho khách xem**.
3. Select **Công khai (Public)**.
4. Set a unique, meaningful public slug (e.g. `dong-ho-nguyen-van`).
5. Choose your Living Person policy (`REDACTED` or `STRICT`).
6. Click **Lưu chế độ hiển thị**.

## 2. Unpublishing
To revoke public access, change the privacy setting back to **Riêng tư (Private)** and save. The public link will immediately stop resolving and return a safe unavailable message.
